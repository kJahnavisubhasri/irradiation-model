Solar Irradiance Prediction

Using Artificial Intelligence to forecast the future of renewable energy.

What is Solar Irradiance?

Solar irradiance is the measure of the power per unit area received from the Sun in the form of electromagnetic radiation. It is essentially the "strength" of the sunlight hitting a specific location at a specific time, measured in Watts per square meter (W/m^2).

Why Predict It?

  • Grid Stability: Helps power plants balance the energy supply between solar and traditional sources.
  • Efficiency: Allows solar farm operators to schedule maintenance during low-light periods.
  • Sustainability: Reduces reliance on "peaker" fossil fuel plants by providing reliable clean energy forecasts.

The Technology

Solar irradiance prediction uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) to process meteorological data. By analyzing variables such as Temperature, Humidity, and Pressure, our models forecast the Sun's intensity.
